A new neighborhood pizzeria

G&A Pizzeria

Pizza from Gianluca Longo — chef and owner of Stefano’s Ristorante — is coming to 840 N. Park Road in Wyomissing, in the old Nino’s Pizza & Pasta building on the Park Road corridor. We open November 2026.

When

Opening November 2026

G&A Pizzeria is registered to the Park Road address and opening this month. Opening-day details and the full menu will be posted right here as soon as they’re set.

Where

840 N. Park Road

The former Nino’s Pizza & Pasta building in Wyomissing, on the N. Park Road corridor about a mile from West Reading — a pizza address Wyomissing already knows.

Who

Chef Gianluca Longo

G&A is the new pizzeria from the chef and owner of Stefano’s Ristorante. It has no connection to the former GNA in West Reading.

What to expect

Pizza first. The rest comes at opening.

G&A is a pizzeria, plain and simple — pies made by a chef who has spent his career in Italian kitchens. The full menu, the opening-day details, and how you’ll order from us will all be announced on this page once the ovens are hot.

Want a heads-up the day we open, or a question about the space, the chef, or the plans? Drop us a note — it goes straight to the team.
