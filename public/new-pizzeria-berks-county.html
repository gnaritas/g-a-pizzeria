The opening

A new pizzeria for Berks County

From registration to the Coming Soon sign to the first pie — how G&A Pizzeria is arriving in Wyomissing this November.

1

The space opens up. Nino’s Pizza & Pasta, a takeaway fixture on N. Park Road, closed its doors in October 2026 — leaving a ready-made pizza storefront in one of Wyomissing’s busiest corridors.

2

The registration lands. In November 2026, G&A Pizzeria registered to the address — 840 N. Park Road — with Gianluca Longo, chef and owner of Stefano’s Ristorante, as the registrant.

3

The sign goes up. Stefano’s channels teased the expansion with a photo of Chef Gianluca holding a Coming Soon sign inside the old Nino’s storefront. The sign reads November 2026.

4

The doors open. Menu, opening-day details, and ordering info post on this site first. Watch this page — or drop us your question now and we’ll answer it before we open.

Not to be confused with

G&A, not GNA

A fair question, since Berks County already had a GNA in West Reading. Per the registration, G&A Pizzeria has no connection to the former GNA. This is a new pizzeria, in Wyomissing, from Stefano’s kitchen.

The full story — chef, timeline, and what’s next — lives on our story page.
