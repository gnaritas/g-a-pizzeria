Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026. This page explains exactly what this website collects and why — nothing else.

What we collect

This site has one place that collects information: the contact form. When you send it, we collect your name, your phone number (if you gave one), your email address (if you gave one), and your message.

Why we collect it

To answer you. Your note goes to the team opening G&A Pizzeria at 840 N Park Road, and we reply by email (or by phone if you left a number and asked for a call). That’s the whole purpose.

What this site does not do

No advertising trackers. No analytics cookies. No mailing-list signups hiding behind a submit button. This site serves you its own files plus the Google map you see on the location pages — when a map loads, Google receives standard request data (like your IP address) under its own privacy policy.

Who we are & how to reach us

G&A Pizzeria, 840 N Park Road, Wyomissing, PA 19610. The fastest way to reach us is the contact form — say “remove my message” and we’ll delete what we have from you.

Retention

We keep contact messages only as long as needed to answer and to remember what we promised you. Ask us to remove your message and we will.

The one-paragraph version

You write to us, we write back. This site doesn’t track you, doesn’t sell your info, and doesn’t sign you up for anything. Questions or removal requests: use the contact form.
