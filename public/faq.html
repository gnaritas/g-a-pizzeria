Frequently Asked Questions

We’re a month from opening, so some answers are honest placeholders. Everything below is confirmed; the rest goes up here the moment it’s set.

When does G&A Pizzeria open?

November 2026. The business registered to 840 N Park Road this November, and the Coming Soon sign in the storefront window reads “November 2026.” Opening-day details post on this site first.

Where is G&A Pizzeria?

840 N Park Road, Wyomissing, PA 19610 — the former Nino’s Pizza & Pasta building on the N. Park Road corridor, about a mile from West Reading. Directions & map →

Who owns G&A Pizzeria?

Gianluca Longo — chef and owner of Stefano’s Ristorante — registered the business to the address in November 2026. Read more on our story page.

Is G&A the same place as GNA in West Reading?

No. G&A Pizzeria has no connection to the former GNA in West Reading. It’s a new pizzeria in Wyomissing, from the kitchen of Stefano’s Ristorante.

What will be on the menu?

G&A is a pizzeria, so pizza is the heart of it. The full menu will be announced when we open — and it will be posted on this website before opening day.

Can I order or reserve right now?

Not yet — the doors aren’t open. Ordering details will be announced before opening day, and the contact form reaches the team in the meantime.
