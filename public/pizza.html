Our Pizza

G&A is built around one thing done right: pizza, from a chef trained in Italian kitchens. The full menu posts here at opening.

What we’re setting up

The kitchen at 840 N. Park Road belongs to Gianluca Longo, chef and owner of Stefano’s Ristorante, and this storefront — the former Nino’s Pizza & Pasta — has been a pizza kitchen for years. G&A keeps that tradition and puts a chef’s hand on it.

We’re announcing the full menu, opening-day hours, and ordering details on this page before we open the doors in November 2026. Until then, the honest answer is: pizza is coming, and everything confirmed gets posted here first.

Have something you hope is on the menu, or a question about the plans? Tell us via the contact page — the notes reach the team directly.

Menu & opening updates

The menu, the hours, and how to order will all be announced here — and on Stefano’s channels — as opening week approaches.
