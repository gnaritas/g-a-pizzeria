Who we are

From Stefano’s Kitchen to Park Road

G&A Pizzeria is a new name — from a chef who already runs one Berks County kitchen.

The chef

Gianluca Longo is the chef and owner of Stefano’s Ristorante — an Italian restaurant and bakery cafe already serving Berks County out of stefanolegacy.com. G&A Pizzeria is his new project: a pizza-focused kitchen registered to 840 N. Park Road in Wyomissing this November.

The expansion was first teased on Stefano’s channels: a photo of Chef Gianluca standing inside the former Nino’s Pizza & Pasta storefront, holding a Coming Soon sign with “November 2026” printed on it. That sign is in the window today.

This isn’t a random tenant moving into a pizza space. The building already knows pizza — Nino’s ran there until October 2026 — and the new chef comes from a serious Italian kitchen. The combination is the whole idea.

The name

One more thing worth clearing up: G&A has no connection to the former GNA in West Reading. Different place, different people, different kitchen. If anyone tells you they’ve “had GNA before” — this is still a brand-new pizzeria, and we’d love to have you try it when we open.
